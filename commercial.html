How It Works

Simple.
Transparent.
On your side.

Most businesses don't realize they can have a dedicated advocate throughout the entire commercial real estate process — at no cost to them. Here's how we work.

01

Requirements

We start with a deep dive into your needs — space size, location, budget, timeline, and what the space needs to do for your business long-term.

02

Search

We survey the market — on-market and off — and filter to the options that actually fit your requirements, so you're not wasting time on tours that go nowhere.

03

Compare

We build a clear side-by-side comparison of your top options — total occupancy cost, lease terms, build-out allowances, and deal-breakers — so the decision is easy.

04

Negotiate

We negotiate hard on your behalf — on rent, tenant improvement allowances, lease length, renewal options, and every clause that protects you if things change.
