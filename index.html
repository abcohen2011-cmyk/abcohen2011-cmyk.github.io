About

Why this,
and why now.

Before real estate, six years in technology gave me a clear lens for systems thinking — how to analyze a problem, work through complexity, and build for the long term rather than the immediate result. After that, nearly five years helping operate a multi-generation furniture manufacturer taught me something different: what it looks like when a business is built to last, and how craftsmanship commands a different kind of respect.

Real estate sits at the intersection of both. It rewards careful analysis, but it also rewards people who understand buildings, neighborhoods, and what makes a space genuinely livable. That combination is what drew me here.

Hammer Realty & Co. is the outcome of those experiences — a real estate company built around representing buyers with complete integrity, acquiring and thoughtfully renovating residential properties, and holding them for the long term. The goal isn't volume. It's making smart decisions over a long horizon.
