How It Works

From first call to signed lease.

01

Tell us what you need

Fill out the form below or call us directly. We'll talk through your budget, move-in timeline, must-haves, and neighborhood preferences. Park City has distinct pockets — Old Town, Prospector, Jeremy Ranch, Snyderville Basin — and we'll help you sort out which one fits your life.

02

We build your shortlist

We search active rentals across all channels and send you a curated shortlist with photos, specs, and our notes on each property. No spam — just the ones that actually match what you told us.

03

Showings — in person or over FaceTime

We schedule and attend your showings — up to 10 are included. If you can't be here, we do a live FaceTime walkthrough at a time that works for your schedule. We'll answer questions in real time and flag anything worth noting.

04

Apply and walk through your lease

Once you've found the right place, we guide you through the application and walk through the lease with you before you sign — terms, move-in fees, renewal language, and anything worth negotiating. Legal questions are referred to an attorney.

Common Questions

Things people ask us.

When is the $499 due?
Due at the start of the engagement — once we've had an initial call and agreed on your search criteria. It covers the full process from search through lease signing.
What if I don't end up renting anything?
We'll work hard to find you the right fit. If the Park City rental market genuinely doesn't have what you need at your budget — which we'll know quickly — we'll tell you honestly. The fee covers the search work; it is not refundable if you ultimately decide not to rent.
Do you handle furnished vacation rentals?
Our focus is longer-term residential rentals — typically 6-month or 12-month leases. For furnished short-term stays, check our Vacation Services, or we can point you toward the right resources.
Can I meet you in person before committing?
Absolutely. We're happy to jump on a video call first at no charge. Most of our rental clients start with a 20-minute intro call before moving forward.
