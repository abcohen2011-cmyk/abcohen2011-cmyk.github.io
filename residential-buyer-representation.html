What We Do

From search to closing — in your corner.

Park City's residential market moves fast. Ski-in/ski-out properties, Old Town condos, Summit Park homes, Snyderville Basin lots — each pocket behaves differently. We help you understand what you're buying before you make an offer.

Property Search
MLS access plus off-market leads and direct outreach. We find homes that match your criteria and flag the ones worth your attention — so you're not wasting weekends on unsuitable showings.
Showings & Tours
In-person showings and FaceTime walkthroughs for buyers relocating from out of state. We know what to look for in Park City's unique housing stock — from snow load on roofs to HOA restrictions.
Offer Strategy
Comparable sales analysis, seller motivation research, and negotiation strategy. We know when to push, when to hold, and how to structure an offer that stands out without overpaying.
Contract & Due Diligence

Trusted inspectors, lenders, title companies, and contractors in Summit County. We'll connect you with the right people — especially if you're coming in from out of state and don't have local contacts.
Close & Beyond
We see you through to closing day — and stay available after. If you have questions in the first months of homeownership, we're a phone call away. This is a relationship, not a transaction.
The Process

From first call to keys in hand.

01

Buyer Consultation

We start with a conversation — your budget, timeline, must-haves, and what's drawing you to Park City. We'll help you sort out which neighborhoods fit your lifestyle, and set honest expectations about what your budget buys in this market.

02

Active Search

We set up your MLS search and start working our network. New listings that match your criteria come to you the moment they hit the market — and we give you our honest read on each one before you make a trip to see it.

03

Showings & Shortlist

We tour properties together — in person or over FaceTime. We're looking for things that don't show up in photos: road noise, sun exposure, deferred maintenance, HOA restrictions, short-term rental rules that might affect resale.

04

Offer & Negotiation

When you're ready to move on a property, we run the comps, talk through offer strategy, and negotiate on your behalf. Park City's market has nuances — HOA rules, ski access easements, STR permit availability — and we know how to position your offer around them.

05

Due Diligence & Close

We coordinate inspections, review disclosures, and walk you through every contingency. If something comes up in due diligence, we help you decide whether to renegotiate, request repairs, or walk away. Then we get you to closing.

Park City Markets

Know the neighborhood before you buy.

Park City isn't one market — it's several, each with different price ranges, HOA rules, and lifestyle tradeoffs. We'll help you understand what you're choosing.

Historic District
Old Town
Walk to Main Street, the gondola, and Park City Mountain Resort. Character homes, condos, and restored miners' cottages. Highest price-per-square-foot in the area; STR permits more available here than in newer neighborhoods.
Mid-Mountain Access
Prospector & Park City Proper
More value per square foot than Old Town while still inside city limits. Good mix of condos, townhomes, and single-family homes. Walkable to the arts district; close to both ski resorts.
Room to Spread Out
Snyderville Basin
Newer construction, larger lots, and a more suburban feel. Excellent schools, proximity to Canyons Village, and easier access to I-80 and Salt Lake City. Popular with families and remote workers.
High-End Resort Living
Deer Valley Adjacent
Luxury condos, ski-in/ski-out properties, and estate homes near Deer Valley Resort. The highest price points in the valley; strong vacation rental history and appeal to second-home buyers.
