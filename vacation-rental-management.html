Questions

What owners usually ask

Do I have to switch my listing to your account?
No. You keep your Airbnb or VRBO listing under your account and receive income directly. We're added as a co-host — which gives us access to manage messages, calendar, and bookings without taking over the listing.
What if I want to use the property myself?
Just let us know your dates and we'll block them on the calendar. Your personal use always takes priority — that's your home first and a rental second.
Do you handle pricing and listing optimization?
We maintain the calendar and can advise on pricing strategy, but major listing changes and pricing decisions stay with you. We're currently focused on co-hosting operations rather than full listing management. As we grow, full management services may follow.
What about cleaning? Do you provide that?
We coordinate your existing cleaning team — scheduling them for every turnover, confirming completion, and handling any issues. We don't supply the cleaners ourselves, but can recommend local teams if you need one.
Is there a minimum contract length?
No. Co-hosting is month to month. We can start quickly and you can end the arrangement with 30 days notice. We'd rather build a long-term relationship than lock you into anything.
Can I also get Home Watch for my property?
Yes, and it works well together. Home Watch visits are typically scheduled between guest stays. Co-hosting clients receive member pricing on Grocery Stocking as well.
